The BOM total for the reconciled scope is USD 388,912 against WP D's USD 381,320 (+2.0%). The COTS propulsion lines are identical because both use the same catalog estimates (the MGM quotes will move them most). The differences:

- **Airframe materials and consumables +12,094:** WP D prices finished CAD mass at USD/kg with 15-25 % scrap; the BOM buys whole stock units: Divinycell H45 in two 2 in sheets for 3.5 kg of spar foam, MGS hardener hazmat/box fees (USD 70 per slow-hardener quart), four EA 9394 kits, carbon tubes as full DragonPlate lengths, and the fasteners and bagging consumables as their own rows (composite USD 30,021, metals 12,205, tubes 5,844, consumables 3,342, hardware 1,940).
- **Tooling materials +3,008:** WP D prices the EPS plugs by finished mass; the plugs are machined from laminated blocks, so the BOM buys the block envelope volume (about 7.3 m3 of XPS boards) and MDF by sheet.
- **Battery -6,680:** WP D used a USD 2,500 per-pack allowance for enclosure, cold plate and bus bars and USD 1/cell for holders and nickel; the BOM itemises G10 holders, copper strip, contactors, fuses, connectors and precharge, and carries the box sheet and cold-plate stock under metals. The mica liner is quote-only and not yet priced.
- **HV, cooling and harness -2,774:** itemised pumps, coolers and an aluminium-cable estimate replace the WP D formula (USD 400 per motor + 1,000 + 30/m).
- **Systems, avionics, cabin +2,474:** Vicor DC-DC converters (250-425 V input, which do not cover the bus) are replaced by four Mean Well RSDH-300-24; oleo dampers, seat track, foam, polycarbonate, latches and pins are added as purchased rows.
- **Outsourced -1,500:** the USD 1,500 AN/MS hardware allowance moved to the hardware rows.
- **Parachute +500:** risers/bridle listed separately (normally part of the Galaxy installation).

Labour is not in either cash figure: WP D's labour hours (incl. tooling and QC, sizing/results/cost_breakdown.md) are broken down by part family in manufacturing/MANUFACTURING.md.
