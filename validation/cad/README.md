# Work package G: CAD verification

Generated by `cad/audit.py` at the end of every `cad/build.py` run. The CAD is built from the iteration-3.3 frozen design (ALL_rpmfree)
(`design/geometry.json`, `design/selected_design.json`), the weight reductions of `design/mass_calibration.json`,
`fea/results/sizing_update.json` and the WP F fitting dimensions (`fea/parts.py`), including the WP F phase-3 changes
and phase 3.2 (redesigned 7075 fuselage clevises, 16 mm main lug eye with 34 mm edge distance, 10 mm tail-clamp
sockets, fused parachute cradle with a 100 x 9 mm beam, H45 layer to |y| 3.26 m, boom over-wraps 0.8/6.7/4.1 mm,
50 x 3.76 mm tail spar, US-stock skid and leg tubes, split floor and side panels); see `cad/README.md`.

## Iteration 3.3 status

36 of 37 clearance checks pass, none fails; the warning is the 1.72 m boom height on the egress path. Pushers
(2 x REB 90, 1.75 m): 0.90 m ground clearance, first strike at 12 deg tail-down, 328 / 250 mm axial gaps behind the
H-tail / fin trailing edges, 1.32 m between the prop tips, 0.80 m behind the rear lift-rotor tip paths. Door 0.49 x
0.67 m (aft edge 50 mm ahead of the rear-spar post), passenger head clearance 75 mm.

Mass/CG: CAD MTOW 1,326.2 kg against 1,350.4 kg (-24.2 kg, -1.8 %), CG 13 mm aft and 12 mm higher; static margin
0.086 against 0.098. Interference: two contacts of 2.4 cm3 each, between the rear-spar clevis cheeks and the aft
corners of the passenger door glazing and the left window; the glazing corners need a local notch. 106 pairs with
non-watertight bodies were not evaluated.

**Resolved in 3.3 (found in 3.2):** the pusher inverter pods are specified under the boom at x = 7.468 m (`pushers[].inverter`),
which is inside the ventral fin (fin x 7.24-7.78 m under the boom) and against the tail clamp; the CAD places them as
specified and the interference scan shows INV-PB / INV-POD-P overlapping VF, RUD, VF-SPAR and TL-CLAMP. Move the pods
ahead of the fin (x <= 7.15 m, between the L4/R4 rotor station and the fin) or package the inverter inside the boom
end ahead of the plug.

Items found while building iterations 3 to 3.2:
- **Battery layout.** The five packs run from x 0.95 to 2.74 m under the cabin floor; the floor runs to the last pack
  and is split into two router-size pieces over an under-floor bulkhead (FLOOR-F/-A + FLOOR-DBL); four under-floor
  bulkheads separate the packs; each pack has its own belly access panel and vent.
- **Pilot leg room fixes the cabin front.** With the pilot at x = 1.15 m the P95 legs reach x ~ 0.49 m, so the forward
  cabin bulkhead (BH-1) is at x = 0.43 m and the avionics bay is only 0.27 m long (BH-0 at 0.16 m).
- **Inverter packaging.** The HBCi inverter envelope does not fit the 116 mm pylon fairing; lift and pusher inverters are
  carried in under-boom pods (INV-POD, INV-POD-P).
- **Flaps over the booms.** Each flap is split at the boom (W-FLAP-I / W-FLAP-O, 2 x 90 mm gap with closing ribs).
- **Hinge line on the rear spar.** The 0.70 c hinge line coincides with the rear spar; the fixed wing ends behind the
  rear-spar caps, so flap and aileron chords are about 30 mm shorter than 0.30 c.
- **PY-C** keeps an 11 % reduction (WP F phase 3) instead of the 30 % in the calibration.
- Minor fit contacts (< 5 cm3) to be notched in detail design: rear-spar clevis legs at the aft edge of the passenger
  door and window, canopy frame against the side-panel top, main-spar lug at the aft canopy edge.
